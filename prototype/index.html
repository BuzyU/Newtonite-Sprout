Frequently asked questions

Straightforward answers before you begin.

What do I need to start investing on Sprout?

You only need three items: your PAN card number, a mobile number linked to your Aadhaar for OTP verification, and an active Indian bank account. All verification is completed digitally.

Why does verification take time?

By financial regulation, your details are cross-referenced with official government registries. This usually takes 45 to 90 seconds. If an agency registry is slow, we save your progress and send you an SMS alert as soon as it clears.

Can I withdraw my money whenever I want?

Yes. Sprout offers liquid and open-ended funds with no minimum lock-in periods. Redemption proceeds typically credit back to your verified bank account within 1 to 3 business days.

Is ₹100 really enough to start?

Yes. Regulatory micro-investing and systematic investment plans (SIPs) allow beginners to start with as little as ₹100 per month.
