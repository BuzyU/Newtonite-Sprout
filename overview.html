1. The Homepage: Fear to Trust in Five Steps

Responsive across 320px, 360px, 390px, 768px, and 1280px with mandatory visible risk disclaimers.

MOBILE FIRST (360px)

Single-Column Narrative & Zero-Scroll Disclaimer

Risk disclaimer (13px) placed directly beneath hero CTA in the first viewport. No auto-playing videos, no deceptive growth tickers, no floating pop-ups.

DESKTOP CONSIDERED (1280px)

Balanced 2-Column Grid & Interactive Preview

Thoughtful desktop layout rather than stretched mobile view. Accommodates the click-to-load video poster, reassurance grid, and native details FAQ.

2. The 5-Step Onboarding Flow

One idea per screen, clear emotional pacing, and Ankur the geometric sprout as a respectful guide.

SCREEN 1: WELCOME

Ankur Greeting & Roadmap

Sets expectations upfront with total time estimate (~7 mins) and clear breakdown of upcoming steps.

SCREEN 2: INTENT

Validating User Hesitation

Offers "I'm just curious" and reassures first-time investors that feeling unsure is completely natural.

SCREEN 3: DOC PREP

Eliminating CS Complaint #1

Lists PAN, Aadhaar phone, and bank details before verification begins, plus a "Remind me later" deferred push permission drawer.

SCREEN 4: KYC VERIFICATION

Consent & Small Steps

Explicit regulatory consent and privacy purpose statement before capturing identity numbers.

SCREEN 5: FIRST ₹100

Plugging the 66.7% Leak

Pre-selected ₹100 default button, plain-language risk notice, and visible first-viewport disclaimer.

RESTRAINED CELEBRATION

Botanical Sprout Growth

Quiet CSS-only sprout growth animation instead of cheap gambling confetti, followed by non-blocking referral credit.

3. Edge States, Error Handling & Localisation

Tested edge cases to safeguard users against drop-off during slow networks and registry delays.

WAIT STATE (CS COMPLAINT #2)

Eliminating "Thought It Crashed"

Reassuring mascot with 45s timer and explicit copy: "You do not need to stare at this screen; we will notify you when complete."

OTP DELAY STATE

Alternative Delivery Channels

Countdown timer and WhatsApp/Call fallback options when SMS gateway latency occurs on government UIDAI portals.

HINDI LOCALISATION DEMO

Natural Conversational Hindi

Tested Devanagari font stack with 30-40% length expansion tolerance, verified line-heights, and zero clipped matras.
